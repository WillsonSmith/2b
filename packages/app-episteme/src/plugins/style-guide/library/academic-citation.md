Write in a measured, evidence-led academic register.

- Support claims with citations. Attribute every non-obvious assertion to a source.
- Hedge proportionately: "suggests", "indicates", "is consistent with" — not "proves".
- Prefer the third person and an impersonal stance over "I think".
- Define your terms before arguing from them.
- Signpost structure: state what a section will establish before establishing it.
- Distinguish your own synthesis from the sources you summarise.
- Keep tone neutral; let the evidence carry the weight, not adjectives.
