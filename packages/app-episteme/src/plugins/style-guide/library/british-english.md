Use British English spelling and conventions throughout.

- Spelling: "-our" (colour, behaviour), "-ise" (organise, recognise), "-re" (centre, theatre), "-lled/-lling" (travelled, modelling).
- Use "whilst", "amongst", and "learnt" where natural.
- Single quotation marks for speech, with double inside: 'She said "no".'
- Place punctuation outside quotation marks unless it belongs to the quote.
- Dates as "5 March 2026". Use the 24-hour clock or "half past ten".
- Collective nouns can take a plural verb: "the team are".
