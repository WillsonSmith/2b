Hold a consistent narrative point of view and keep prose immersive.

- Stay in the chosen POV. Don't slip into a head the viewpoint character can't access.
- Keep tense consistent — past or present, not both.
- Favour concrete sensory detail over abstract summary.
- Show emotion through action and subtext rather than naming it.
- Vary sentence rhythm: short beats land; long sentences build.
- Cut adverbs that prop up weak verbs. "Sprinted", not "ran quickly".
- Keep dialogue tags simple — "said" is usually enough.
