Use punctuation deliberately; don't lean on the em dash as a catch-all.

- Reserve the em dash for a genuine break in thought or an emphatic aside. At most one per paragraph.
- Prefer a comma, colon, or full stop where one will do the job.
- Use a colon to introduce a list or an explanation that completes the clause.
- Use parentheses for a true aside the sentence could drop without loss.
- Don't chain dashes to dodge sentence structure — rewrite the sentence instead.
- Keep spacing consistent: no spaces around the em dash.
