Write so a non-expert understands on the first read.

- Choose the everyday word over the formal one: "use" not "utilize", "buy" not "purchase", "help" not "facilitate".
- Spell out an acronym the first time it appears, then use it freely.
- Explain a term before you lean on it. Don't assume shared jargon.
- Prefer concrete examples to abstract description.
- Keep the subject and verb close together near the start of the sentence.
- Address the reader directly as "you" where it fits.
